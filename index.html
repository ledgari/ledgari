Insights

Your numbers, explained in plain words.

Ledgari watches your sales, stock and customers for you. Each insight says what changed, what it's costing you, and gives you a next step.

  • Know when sales fall at a store, compared with the same days last week
  • Find stock that hasn't sold in weeks, and how much cash it's holding
  • Spot regular customers who've gone quiet, and win them back with one email
  • See your busiest hours so you can plan staff and stock
